### Conversations in the web console
- Server admins can turn on web transcripts for each channel. Off by default.
- In the console, open a channel or task thread to read its transcript. Discord permissions decide who can see it, checked live.
- Open a Slopbot reply to see its run: streamed text, thinking and tool calls. New replies stream live.
- Reply from the web. Your message shows in Discord with your name and avatar, and Slopbot always answers it.

### Usage limits
- Each person has a 5-hour and a weekly usage limit, based on the cost of the AI calls their messages cause.
- A reply that starts under the limit can finish. Past the limit, Slopbot stops using tools and gives a short final answer.
- Use the `usage` command to see your usage and to use a banked reset.

### Share links
- Admins can share a frozen copy of a conversation. It leaves out user IDs, cost and tool results.
